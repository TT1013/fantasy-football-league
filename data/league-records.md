# TT13's Fantasy Challenge — League Records

Canonical historical record. The Canton site reads from this file; do not maintain a
second copy of these numbers anywhere else.

## Conventions

- **A season is named for the January it ends in.** The "2021 season" was played
  September–December 2020 and concluded 7 January 2021.
- **A champion wins two powers**: to amend one rule, and to name the following chapter.
  A declaration made after winning season *N* governs season *N+1*.
- `*` in a standings table marks a playoff berth. Final rank reflects playoff finish,
  not regular-season record.

### Reading the numbers

- **Tommy Tran has the best all-around résumé that isn't Binh's.** Best win% among
  multi-season managers, the only 100% playoff rate in the league (6 for 6), and the
  largest average scoring margin — yet only one ring to Binh's two. The gap between
  "best process" and "most titles" is real and belongs on his manager page.
- **maniel scores more than anyone, in fewer seasons than anyone else at the top.**
  2093.40 PF/season is the league's highest average, in just 4 years — the shortest
  tenure of any multi-season manager with a title.
- **Binh Nguyen's two titles came from middling regular-season output.** 1989.63 PF/season
  trails both Tommy's and maniel's, and his average margin is modest for a two-time
  champion. Both rings were won as lower seeds (Chapter I as a 7th-in-points team,
  Chapter IV as a 4-seed) — the numbers back up what the chapter notes already said:
  he wins in January, not September.
- **Ben Thai has the league's worst playoff rate by a wide margin** — 16.7%, a single
  playoff berth in six seasons — despite a losing percentage only marginally worse than
  Phong Nguyen's.
- **Dan Holmqvist's average conceals the story rather than telling it.** −129.08 average
  margin is the league's worst, driven almost entirely by a 4-10 Chapter V — and he's also
  the reigning champion. Small sample, large variance; worth a note on his page rather
  than a raw ranking.
- **Eddie Fisher is the league's most neutral manager statistically** — PF and PA nearly
  identical (2001.31 vs 1999.02) across three seasons, a coin-flip scoring differential.
