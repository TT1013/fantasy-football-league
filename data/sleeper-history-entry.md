# Sleeper — Manual League History entry sheet

Mobile app only: League → Commissioner → League History → add a season.
Fields accepted: **Season year · Top 3 finishers · Regular Season Standings · League Note**.

> Enter the **NFL season year** (2020–2024), not the league's own chapter naming.
> Chapter VI (NFL 2025) was played on Sleeper and is already there — do not re-enter it.
> Standings below are **regular-season order** (wins, then points), which differs from the
> final placements in our records — those reflect playoff finish.
